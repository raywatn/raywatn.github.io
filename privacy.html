Privacy

FlyStay's basic search form runs in your browser. The information you enter is used to build a search link and is sent to the travel provider only when you choose to open that provider's website.

This starter website does not include user accounts, payment processing, analytics or a database. The hosting provider and third-party travel sites may process technical data under their own privacy policies.

Before adding analytics, advertising pixels, newsletter forms or other services, update this notice to explain what data is collected and how it is used.
